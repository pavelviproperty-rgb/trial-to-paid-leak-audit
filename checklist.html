Trial-to-paid leak check

Use this before buying tooling or changing your trial funnel. The goal is to separate real trial churn from first-charge payment failure and app access-state drift. You can run the first pass from redacted exports and screenshots before connecting any Stripe app.

1. Split the funnel

  • Trial started.
  • Trial ended.
  • First paid invoice attempted.
  • First paid invoice failed.
  • First paid invoice later paid or never recovered.
  • Normal recurring renewal failed later.

2. Pull safe evidence

  • Trial starts per month.
  • Trial length and first-month price.
  • First-charge failure rate.
  • Decline-code counts.
  • Retry count and next retry date.
  • Recovered_at or paid_at.
  • Payment method updated or not.
  • Customer active in app during the failure window.

3. Check app access state

  • What happens when `invoice.payment_failed` fires?
  • Does the webhook update the user or subscription row, or only log the event?
  • Does the app keep access while the invoice is `past_due`?
  • Does access restore automatically when the invoice is later paid?
  • Can support see the mismatch without opening Stripe and the app database separately?

4. Do not send sensitive data

Redact customer names, emails, card details, API keys, passwords, and anything that identifies a person. The first pass should work from screenshots, exports, counts, and a plain-English description of app access logic. Do not connect Stripe OAuth or share unrestricted account access just to classify the leak.
