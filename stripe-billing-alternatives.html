The fast decision

Switch providers only after you know which failure bucket is actually costing money. A gateway can improve routing or authorization, but it will not fix unclear app access state, webhook gaps, or first-trial invoice reporting by itself.

Processor problems

  • High decline rates from a specific country, issuer, or card type.
  • Authorization or routing costs that make retries expensive.
  • Payment methods your current stack does not support well.
  • Fraud rules blocking legitimate cards before recovery can begin.

State and recovery problems

  • First post-trial invoice failures mixed with normal renewal failures.
  • No view of payment failed -> card update started -> invoice paid.
  • Product access stays active, pauses too early, or never restores.
  • Webhook success does not prove your app updated entitlement state.

The 30-minute no-OAuth audit table

  • Subscription or invoice id, redacted.
  • Failure bucket: first invoice after trial, normal renewal, or manual/admin state.
  • Decline code, retry count, next attempt, and paid/recovered outcome.
  • Card update started, card updated, or no customer action.
  • App access state at failure, during retry, and after recovery.
  • Whether access state came from webhook, scheduled reconcile, or manual support.
