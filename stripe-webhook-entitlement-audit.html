The fast diagnosis

The expensive bug is rarely just "Stripe failed". It is usually mismatch: Stripe invoice state says one thing, your subscription row says another, and product access follows a third source of truth.

Leak cases

  • invoice.payment_failed updates nothing because Stripe will retry anyway.
  • invoice.paid succeeds but product access never restores.
  • customer.subscription.updated overwrites a more accurate invoice state.
  • A scheduled reconcile job later reverts the correct access state.

What the audit compares

  • Invoice/subscription state in Stripe.
  • Webhook event received and handler result.
  • App subscription row before and after the event.
  • Product access state before failure, during retry, and after recovery.

The no-OAuth evidence packet

  • One redacted invoice/subscription timeline.
  • Webhook logs for payment failed, payment paid, and subscription updated events.
  • Screenshot or export of the app user/subscription row at the same timestamps.
  • A short note describing when access pauses, graces, restores, or cancels.
  • No card data, API keys, customer personal data, or unrestricted Stripe login.

When this fits

  • You use Stripe Billing or subscriptions.
  • You rely on webhooks to pause or restore access.
  • You have failed payments, trial conversions, or renewal failures.
  • Customers sometimes keep access after payment failure.
  • Recovered customers sometimes stay locked out.
  • Your dashboard cannot show invoice state and access state side by side.
