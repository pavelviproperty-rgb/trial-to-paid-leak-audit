The missing layer

Metered billing solves usage ingestion and invoicing. It does not automatically decide when a workflow should hard-stop, when quota should change, when access should restore, or how to explain margin by workflow after retries and overages.

Billing truth

What Stripe billed, retried, or marked paid or unpaid.

Usage truth

What the product actually ran, logged, retried, or allowed.

Access truth

What the customer could still do after hitting limits or payment-state changes.

What this audit checks

  • Whether usage can continue after the workflow should hard-stop.
  • Whether overages, credits, or quotas version cleanly across plans.
  • Whether retries and restore-access logic are aligned with billing state.
  • Whether the product can explain one row from usage event to paid outcome.
  • Whether metered billing is masking a product-state or entitlement leak.
